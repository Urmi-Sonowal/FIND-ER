Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long are found items stored?

Found items are securely stored for up to 30 days as per college policy.

What ID is required for collection?

A valid college ID is mandatory. Additional verification may be required.

Can I report after office hours?

Yes. The Find-ER portal is available 24/7 for reporting.

Is there any service charge?

No. The lost & found service is completely free.
