Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about reporting, claiming and using TraceIt.

How do I report a lost item?

Click "Report" in the navigation, fill the form with details and upload a photo if available. We'll notify you when there's a potential match.

How can I claim a found item?

If an item is marked Ready for Pickup, visit the Lost & Found office with your College ID. The Office may ask additional verification questions.

What if I don't have a photo?

Provide as many distinguishing details as possible: color, brand, markings, and exact location/time when the item was lost.

How long are items stored?

Found items are stored for up to 30 days. Per college policy, unclaimed items beyond that may be disposed of or donated.

Is my personal data safe?

Yes — TraceIt stores only necessary contact details for reporting and retrieval. We do not share personal data without consent.

Who do I contact for technical issues?

Contact IT support at support@college.edu or use the Contact page to submit a message.

Still have questions?

Use the contact form on the Contact page and we'll get back within 1 working day.
