Report Your Item

Fill out a simple form with details about your lost or found item. Include the item name, category, location, date, and a clear photo if available. The more details you provide, the better chance of matching!

Verification Process

Our campus admin team reviews your report to ensure accuracy and prevent false claims. This usually takes less than 24 hours. Once verified, your report becomes visible to others.

💡 Tip: Check your notifications for status updates
03
🔄

Smart Matching

Our intelligent system automatically matches lost items with found reports based on category, location, description, and timing. You'll receive instant notifications when a potential match is found!

💡 Tip: Enable notifications to never miss a match
04
🎉

Recover Your Item

Once your item is ready for pickup, visit the Lost & Found office with your College ID. Our staff will verify your identity and hand over your recovered item. It's that simple!

💡 Tip: Bring your College ID and any proof of ownership

FAQ

Common Questions

❓ How long does it take to get a match? ▼

Match times vary depending on the item. ID cards and electronics are often matched within 24-48 hours, while other items may take 3-5 days.

❓ Can I report on behalf of someone else? ▼

Yes! You can report a found item on behalf of someone. For lost items, the owner should ideally report themselves.

❓ Is there a fee for using TraceIt? ▼

No! TraceIt is completely free for all students and staff. It's a campus service provided by the college.
