User Instructions

Step-by-step guides to report, claim, and manage your items on TraceIt.

Reporting a Lost Item

  1. Click Report from the navigation.
  2. Choose Lost, provide the item name, category and the location where it was lost.
  3. Upload a clear photo (if available) and add distinguishing details.
  4. Submit the form — we'll notify you when a potential match is found.

Reporting a Found Item

  1. Click Report and choose Found.
  2. Describe where you found it, approximate time, and upload a photo.
  3. Provide a contact number or leave it anonymous for initial posting.
  4. The lost owner will be notified if a match is identified.

Claiming an Item

  1. When an item is marked Ready for Pickup, visit the Lost & Found office.
  2. Bring your College ID and any supporting proof that it's your item.
  3. The office will verify and hand over the item upon successful validation.

Managing Your Reports

  1. Go to your Profile to view all your reports.
  2. You can edit or cancel an active report if the item is found.
  3. Use the notifications panel to stay updated on matches.

Tips for Faster Recovery

  • Upload clear photos and describe unique marks.
  • Report the item as soon as possible to improve match chances.
  • Check notifications and email for match alerts regularly.
